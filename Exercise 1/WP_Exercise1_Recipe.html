Recipe for Chocolate Brownies!

These brownies are rich, dense, and intensely chocolatey with a soft, fudgy center and a slightly crisp top. They’re simple to prepare and ideal when you want a reliable dessert.

The Ingredients:

  • 200g dark chocolate (roughly chopped)
  • 150g unsalted butter
  • 200g granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 100g all-purpose flour
  • 30g cocoa powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

    • The Method:

    Now that you have all the ingredients, let's get started.

    1. Prepare the oven and pan
    Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F). Line a square baking tin (about 20cm) with parchment paper.
    2. Melt chocolate and butter
    In a heatproof bowl, gently melt the dark chocolate and butter together over a water bath or in short microwave bursts. Stir until smooth, then let it cool slightly.
    3. Mix wet ingredients
    Add the sugar to the melted mixture and stir well. Beat in the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla extract, until the mixture is glossy and thick.
    4. Incorporate dry ingredients
    Sift in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt. Fold gently until just combined—avoid overmixing to keep the texture fudgy.
    5. Bake
    Pour the batter into the prepared tin and spread evenly. Bake for 20–25 minutes, or until the edges are set but the center remains slightly soft.
    6. Cool and serve
    Allow the brownies to cool completely in the pan before slicing. This helps them set and develop their signature fudgy texture.
